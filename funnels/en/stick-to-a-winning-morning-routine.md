## Email 2 (giorno 2)

**Oggetto:** Quick Tip: Boost Your Morning Energy

Hey again! A small tweak can make a big difference in how you feel after you get out of bed. Try placing a glass of water on your nightstand and drinking it the moment your alarm goes off. Hydration jump‑starts your metabolism, clears morning grogginess, and signals your brain that it’s time to awaken. Pair this with a quick stretch—reach for the ceiling, roll your shoulders, and take three deep breaths. The movement awakens muscles and improves circulation, giving you a natural energy lift without caffeine. Add this two‑step habit to your existing cue, and you’ll notice a clearer mind and steadier focus within minutes. Give it a try tomorrow and feel the difference!
