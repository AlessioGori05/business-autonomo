**Oggetto:** One Trick to Double Your Focus

Hey, Quick tip for you today. Most people think they need more time to learn, but they actually need better focus. Here is a simple technique called the 'Pomodoro Method.' Set a timer for 25 minutes and work on one specific sub-skill without any distractions. When the timer goes off, take a five-minute break. After four cycles, take a longer break. This method helps your brain stay fresh and prevents burnout. Try it with your next practice session. You will likely find that you retain more information in 25 focused minutes than in two hours of distracted effort. It is about quality, not just quantity. Give it a try and see the difference.
