## Email 2 (giorno 2)

**Oggetto:** The 5-Second Trick for Starting

Hi! Quick tip for you today. When you feel the urge to procrastinate, your brain is trying to protect you from discomfort. To bypass this, use a simple counting technique. Count backwards from five to one. When you hit one, physically move. This interrupts the automatic hesitation loop in your brain. It’s not magic, it’s just a mental reset. Try it the next time you avoid an email or a chore. You’ll be surprised how easy it is to start once you break the initial inertia. Consistency beats intensity. Just keep showing up for those small moments.

**Oggetto:** How I Stopped Waiting for Motivation

Hi! I used to wait for the 'right mood' to work. I’d spend hours planning, but never executing. Then I realized motivation is often a result of action, not the cause. I started forcing myself to do just two minutes of work. Usually, that was enough to get my gears turning. If I didn’t want to continue, I allowed myself to stop. But most of the time, I stayed. This shifted my identity from someone who waits to someone who acts. You don’t need to feel ready. You just need to begin. What’s one two-minute task you can do right now? Try it before you close this email.
