## Email 1 (giorno 0)

**Oggetto:** Welcome! Your 7‑Day Declutter Sprint Starts Now

Hi there! I’m thrilled you’ve joined the 7‑Day Declutter Sprint. Over the next week you’ll receive short, actionable steps that fit into a busy schedule, plus a quick checklist to keep you on track. Today’s focus is the entryway – the first impression of your home. By gathering everything into a basket, deciding what stays, and creating a simple home for keys and mail, you’ll instantly see a cleaner welcome area. This small win builds momentum for the days ahead. Keep the guide handy, set a timer for 15‑20 minutes, and enjoy the satisfaction of a tidier space. I’m here to cheer you on, so feel free to reply with any questions!

## Email 2 (giorno 2)

**Oggetto:** Day 2 tip: Keep kitchen counters clear

A clear countertop is the foundation of an efficient kitchen. Start by removing every item, then return only the tools you truly use each day, such as a coffee maker or a cutting board. Group similar items—spices, oils, utensils—inside a drawer organizer to free up space and make cooking smoother. Finally, place a small inbox on the counter for bills or grocery lists and commit to clearing it each evening. This routine takes just a few minutes but dramatically reduces visual clutter and makes meal prep more enjoyable. Try it tonight and notice how much calmer the kitchen feels!
